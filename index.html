First launch

Windows

The installer is not code-signed yet. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", choose More info, then Run anyway. ChatDeck installs for you alone and updates itself from Settings.

Mac

Open the dmg and drag ChatDeck to Applications. The app is not notarized yet, so macOS blocks the first launch: open System Settings, Privacy & Security, and choose Open Anyway. If it says the app "is damaged" instead, run xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/ChatDeck.app in Terminal once. Updates are manual for now.

ChatDeck works with ChatGPT and Claude and needs your own account with whichever you use. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by OpenAI or Anthropic.
